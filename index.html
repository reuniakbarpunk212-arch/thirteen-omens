The Thirteenth Almanac

Being a true & faithful register of the Thirteen Omens — 3,333 tokens of fortune and misfortune, struck upon the BNB Smart Chain for those who read the signs.

Printed on-chain · No waitlist · The edition closes when the last omen is taken

Every almanac warns its reader, and this one is no different. Thirteen omens have been entered into the ledger. Some are lucky, some are not, and the thirteenth is blank — it bears the number of whoever claims it. One thousand five hundred omens are given away: 1,000 to bearers of a Binance Agent ID and 500 to verified accounts of X, one apiece, first come first served. The remainder are sold at 0.001 BNB each, and every coin passes straight to the treasury in the same transaction — the Almanac keeps nothing, holds nothing, and promises nothing.
